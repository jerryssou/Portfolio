Source Gallery

« Capturing the beauty of Source Engine level design, and way beyond. »

Source Gallery is a media created to share the most impressive-looking maps and environments, especially those created by the community, on X and TikTok (until the opening of my own website), through a weekly schedule covering GoldSrc, Source and Source 2, with different themes and games featured each week.

Several other projects are currently in the early stages of development, in collaboration with prominent members of the Source community, with the goal of expanding Source Gallery into something much bigger than just a gallery.

It has now become one of the largest media related to level design and, more broadly, to everything made on Valve's engines, reaching several million views every month. Source Gallery also celebrated its first anniversary on July 19, 2026.

Since this anniversary, Source Gallery has expanded to also act as a platform to share what is currently being made by the community, not only for level design, but for everything related to all three of Valve's engines.

The website will be a sanctuary whose goal will be to host, forever, this art, made with love by the community.
