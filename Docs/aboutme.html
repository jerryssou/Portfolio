I am Jerryssou

A French level designer specialized in the creation of environments and large maps on the Source Engine, especially in Garry's Mod.

Origins

My level design journey on Source started in March 2024, when I tried to edit a map for a friend's server. This attempt was, obviously, too ambitious for a beginner, and I quickly distanced myself from Hammer, launching it from time to time to make a couple of maps that were only for testing purposes.

In April 2025, on my birthday, I decided to give Hammer a second try, with the idea of recreating a place that I often see in real life. The map ended up being way larger than I expected, and was totally changed to fit the universe that I like the most in video games: Half-Life 2. It was on this map that I started to see the potential that level design had for me, and how much I loved doing it.

First success

One month later, in May 2025, I won 2 competitions: one organised by the HEDG (Hammer Editor Development Group), the largest community dedicated to level design worldwide, and the other one by the Mapping Store, the largest community dedicated to level design in France, thanks to two entries that I worked on at the same time for 1 week, "Hoffman House" and "Rivière". These 2 events became the turning point of my journey, and I knew that level design deserved way more of my time.

Half-Life: Alyx and Source Gallery

During the summer of 2025, my goal was to create a map that only used the assets from Half-Life: Alyx in Garry's Mod. But for that, I had to port everything by myself, since nobody had done it before back in 2025. This is where I picked up multiple pieces of software that I had never used before, including Blender, GIMP, Photoshop, Crowbar, Source2Viewer, SourceIO and so on.

Despite some great results, I decided to give up on this project, due to the massive amount of work required to complete it, but also because the gap in quality between Source and Source 2 is too large to be acceptable. However, thanks to this project, I ended up creating the media Source Gallery at the end of July, which was at first created only to share pictures of Half-Life: Alyx. Thanks to an unexpected success, I now share through this media everything related to level design on the 3 engines of Valve. I am now proud to say that Source Gallery has become one of the most influential and active media related to Source, behind major figures of this community such as LambdaGeneration, Tyler McVicker or GabeFollower.

City 26 V2

In August 2025, while still working a little on the Half-Life: Alyx project, I was recruited by the server that made me discover the Half-Life 2 Roleplay universe, in order to edit an already existing map. This edit, which I estimated would take 1 or 2 months at most, ended up taking 7 full months of work on this map alone, released at the end of February 2026. Thanks to it, I discovered and learned pretty much everything that I know about Hammer, Source, and its limitations. This map wasn't a commission, and was made 100% by myself, with my own ideas. This is why it took me so much time, but it was worth it, despite not being used as much as I expected and not earning a single cent from it.

First commissions

After finishing City 26 V2, I originally planned to keep editing this map a little more to obtain what I was really looking for, but I was quickly contacted by a friend of mine who saw my work and needed a map for his server, which I made from the end of March to the end of April. After this one, many other commissions came from multiple server owners, and I also kept editing City 26 V2, as well as starting 2 other Half-Life 2 Roleplay maps on the side during the summer of 2026.

Now

Right now, I'm working on 3 maps: Outland Desert, Sector 1045 and Fallen World. Outland Desert is nearly done, while the 2 others are still under heavy work. You can learn more about these 3 maps and the others by going to the gallery.

What's next?

My goal for the future is to use the knowledge that I have acquired in architecture and programming through my studies, and the things that I have learned on my own in Hammer, in order to make what I always wanted to do when I started mapping: recreations of real places that I find interesting, with assets created from scratch by myself.

With this goal in mind, one of my other projects would be to expand the universe of Half-Life into something that I always wanted to see coming from Valve: an adventure multiplayer game set in the Quarantine Zone from Half-Life: Alyx. With the Half-Life: Alyx IP potentially being acquired by Facepunch for S&box, this project might become reality one day.

To do so, I plan on moving to Source 2 and using S&box as soon as I have finished my studies and the work that I have started on Source.

For Source Gallery, many projects are also being worked on. One of them is a website that is slowly but surely taking shape.
